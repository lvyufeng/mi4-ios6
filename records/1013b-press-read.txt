2. THE REMAINING BLOCKER: the control leaf reads APX-set (wcapx=1)
================================================================================
`wcleaf=0x8008021a`, decoded: bits[1:0]=0b10 (L2 small page), bit4 (L2_ACCESS_PRW 0x10) set, bit5
(L2_ACCESS_USER 0x20) clear, bit9 (L2_ACCESS_APX 0x200) SET. So AP[2:0] = (1,0,1) = user READ-ONLY,
`L2_ACCESS_PRO 0x210` shape-with-APX. The design PREDICTED the control VA `0x80080000` would be `L2_ACCESS_PRW`
(0x10, no APX) because `arm_vm_init.c:195/226` maps the identity range with `*ptv |= L2_ACCESS_PRW`. It does
not: at the storm this page reads user-RO.

The checker's control rule (`check_1013_capture.py`: `wck!=1 or wcapx!=0 or wcoow==1` => bad) fires on
`wcapx!=0`, so `WIT_CLASS="WITNESS_METHOD_BROKEN"` and V1-V3 are WITHHELD. **The fix moved the failure from
"the control cannot read at all" to "the control reads a real leaf whose access bits differ from the design's
prediction."** Whether `wcapx=1` is a REAL device value (the page is kernel text, later mapped user-RO by
`pmap_init`/`pmap_protect` — cf. 979's data-NX loop) or a PROBE defect is the open question (adjudication agent).
