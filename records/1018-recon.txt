## 0. THE FINDING IN ONE LINE

**The live channel hit its cap. `__wrap_sleh_undef` — the recorder that would have named the
`udf #254` — writes through the CAPPED channel and therefore wrote NOTHING, while the frontier
it records happened ~4000 records later on the DIRECT channel.** 1017 named the instruction
statically; this is why the capture could not name it dynamically.

## 1. THE TWO CHANNELS, AND WHICH RECORDS SURVIVE THE CAP

`src/entry/entry_stubs.c` carries two writers against the same 2 MB RAM console:

  entry_live_write(key, value)   :4441  — CAPPED. Drops every record once
                                          `g_live_records >= ENTRY_LIVE_CAP` (:4254, **24576u**),
                                          one `xnu_live_capped` marker then silence.
  entry_write_kv(key, value)     :4476  — DIRECT. No record-count gate; the only bound is the
                                          console's own `max = 0x00200000u - 12u` (:4019).

The capture says exactly one thing about the cap (`grep -n xnu_live_capped`):

  :29963   xnu_live_capped=0x00006000        (0x6000 = 24576 = ENTRY_LIVE_CAP)

**So: every `entry_live_write` record AFTER line 29963 was silently dropped.**

## 2. THE THREE RECORDS THAT PROVE IT — read after the cap, and they are the frontier

  a. `xnu_live_sleh_seq` stops at **`0x00000028` = 40** (last at :28973/:28988), which is
     BEFORE the cap. **This arm has `STAGE90_XNU_ABORT_TRACE=1`** (out/stage90/xnu_arm_entry-config.txt),
     which sets `SLEH_LIVE_MAX`/`FRAMES`/`SEEN` all to **512** (entry_stubs.c:1728-1730) — so
     the sleh band was 512, and the record stopped at 40 NOT because its band cut it. It
     stopped because `entry_live_write` was already dropping.
  b. `xnu_live_undef_seq` appears **zero** times in the whole capture (`grep -c` = 0).
  c. `xnu_live_abt_nfar` reaches **`0x000003bf` = 959**, and **921 of its 959 records are
     AFTER the cap line** (`awk 'NR>29963'`). The abort recorder kept writing; the abort
     *detail* recorder (`sleh`) did not.

  `xnu_live_abt_nfar` counts **CHANGES in `far`** (`entry_stubs.c:2079`), not aborts; the
  abort count is `xnu_live_abt_pcn`/`_farn`'s ladder, whose max published row is `0x00000200`
  = 512 (the same 512 `records/1016a-read.txt` §1 reads). **⚠ CORRECTION TO 1016a §1/§7:**
  1016a cites "`xnu_live_abt_farn` max = `0x3bf` = 959" — that value belongs to `_nfar`
  (distinct-`far` changes), not `_farn`. 1016a's stated meaning ("959 aborts, down from 2.1 M")
  is right in substance; the key name it attributes it to is wrong. Non-load-bearing to its
  verdict (the storm is absent by the 11/11 distinct-`far` argument either way).

### 2.1 THE ARITHMETIC RECONCILES — the wrapper ran ~961 times, `sleh` logged 40

  Both `entry_note_sleh` (:5829) and `entry_note_abt_far`/`_pc` (:5852/:5861) are called from
  the SAME wrapper, `__wrap_sleh_abort` (entry_trace.c:5742-5895), so **the wrapper's entry
  count bounds both**. Read by value:

  - `_pcn` is a ladder over the CALL ordinal: `n <= 4 || n a power of two` (:2056). Its last
    published row is `0x200 = 512`; the next would be `1024` and is **absent** ⇒ the wrapper
    was entered in **`(512, 1024)`** times (the same window argument as `USVC_FULL_N`, and the
    same trap: a ladder's top row is NOT a frontier).
  - `_nfar` = **959** changes of `far` ⇒ ~959 distinct faulting addresses ⇒ ~961 entries.
    **They agree**, and both are consistent with the ladder's missing `1024` row.
  - `_sleh_seq` = **40** and monotone 1…40 ⇒ **only 40 `entry_note_sleh` records reached the
    console**, out of ~961 calls.

  **The gap is exactly the cap.** `sleh` writes on the capped channel; `abt` writes direct.
  The 40th `sleh` is at :28988 and the cap marker at :29963 — every `sleh` write BEFORE the
  cap survived, every one after was dropped, while `abt` kept writing (921 of its 959 `_nfar`
  records are past the cap). **This is the whole defect: the same abort is recorded by two
  recorders, one capped and one not, and the capped one is the one that carries the frame.**

## 3. WHAT THE CAP HIDES — THE UNDEF WITNESS

1017 (`docs/1017-shared-cache-naming.md`) named, statically from the cache artifact:

  un-slid VA `0x3823aa54` = `libsystem_platform.dylib` `__os_alloc_slow` + 0x108
                          = the Thumb `udf #254` (`bytes fede`) reached ONLY when
                            `_mach_vm_map` returns non-zero.

The DYNAMIC recorder for exactly that instruction already exists in this tree:
`__wrap_sleh_undef` (entry_trace.c:5537), installed by `--wrap=sleh_undef`
(build_entry.sh:197), reached from Apple's user undef vector
`undef_from_user -> bl __wrap_sleh_undef` (linked image: `8001cf34`, verified by
`arm-none-eabi-objdump -d out/stage90/xnu_arm_entry.elf`). It records `_pc`/`_lr`/`_spsr`/
`_user` at the first four entries, plus `_seq`.

**It fired zero times in the capture** — not because no `udf` happened, but because by then
its channel was capped. The `udf` handler itself is proven working: the boot self-test
`stage90_xnu_arm_vm_init_high_va_undef_handler_undef_handled=0x1` (capture :3480).

**⇒ THIS IS THE MEASUREMENT DEFECT CLASS, AGAIN ([[mi4-measurement-defects]]): a record whose
ABSENCE was read as evidence was capped.** 1017 §3.3 wrote `# UNMEASURED` for "why the pc
never advances"; the capture cannot answer it because the instrument that would have was
silenced by a counter, and the stall sampler (direct channel) is the only survivor.

## 5. THE RUNG-1018 HYPOTHESIS THIS SPAWNS

If pid 1 is wedged at a `udf` that XNU's own undef path would triage into
`EXC_BAD_INSTRUCTION`, then a **re-run with the cap lifted (or the undef witness moved to the
DIRECT channel) converts the `# UNMEASURED` of 1017 §3.3 into a named mechanism**:

  (A) the undef vector fires and `sleh_undef` takes the USER arm → the run should have died
      (the user arm ends in `exception_triage`), but it did NOT die (resident, `wdt_pets`
      climbing). That combination is itself the measurement: **the trap is reached but the
      handler does not terminate the process** — consistent with the 1010–1015
      "success reported, fault re-executes" shape, now in USER mode at a `udf`.
  (B) the undef vector does NOT fire → the pc is not being executed as a `udf` at all, and
      1017's static identification is contradicted by the dynamic one (the honest
      falsification; a named-but-wrong instruction).

**A and B are disjoint and jointly exhaustive.** Neither is decided by this capture; both need
the cap lifted so the capped-channel witness can speak. This is 1018's arm design input.
