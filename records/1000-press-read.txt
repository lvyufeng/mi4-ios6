## Root cause: the one unguarded clause in vm_map_enter's tail

The call at vm_map.c:2149-2161 is guarded by `object != VM_OBJECT_NULL && vm_map_pmap_enter_enable
&& !anywhere && !needs_copy && size < 128K` - with NO is_submap guard. On a SUBMAP enter the
`object` parameter is the submap itself (`:2063 submap = (vm_map_t) object;`) - a vm_map, not a
vm_object - so vm_map_pmap_enter locks and probes a vm_map AS IF it were a vm_object:
vm_object_lock lands on the element's +8 word (the lock of a struct vm_object) and vm_page_lookup
reads its +0x18 (memq_hint). At launchd's exec two submap enters (<128K, needs_copy FALSE) run
this path; 999's guard records prove it reaching the lookup; 1000's lock word held 0xffb5be0b
(bit0 set) => hw_lock_lock's panic.
The SAME function guards the SAME parameter 30 lines later - `:2192 if (!is_submap &&
object != VM_OBJECT_NULL && object->named && ...)` for the pager block. The missing clause is
that idiom. All four 2050-era trees in this workspace carry the unguarded call verbatim (pristine
apple-xnu-rel-2050 :2143, xnu-2050.18.24 :2143, xnu-upstream :2143, hd2-darwin13 :2149). The
xnu-4570.1.46 tree in-tree carries only bsd/; its osfmk/vm/vm_map.c was fetched raw (462 KB, four
windows): `vm_map_pmap_enter` appears only in an `__unused` forward declaration, and no call site
surfaced in the ~400 KB the fetcher surfaced (in-window truncation caveat) - consistent with
Apple retiring the call after 2050, but NOT load-bearing: the guard stands on this reproduction.

## Why 999 lived and this press died

Same code, same two freed elements, same caller. 999's elements happened to hold benign words
(vm_object_lock succeeded; the 991 memq_hint guard then caught the lookup and the boot went on to
user mode); 1000's +8 held 0xffb5be0b. 990, 991-999 and 1000 have now died OR been guarded at
three different fields of the same two non-objects (+0x18 load in 990, +0x18 guard in 991-999,
+0x8 lock in 1000). The call is the common cause; skipping it for submaps removes all three
exposures and makes the rung independent of what DRAM happens to hold.
