CORRECTED MECHANISM
-------------------
  The shim already implements the mach_port.c:518 fast-trap-with-fallback:
  trap 24, then 0x381cdf40 if it returns 0x10000003.  So if trap 24 were ever
  dispatched, swi_mach would panic (c) -- the run would end at the trap, not at
  the udf.  It did not.  The wrapper's udf at 3824e022 is therefore fed by the
  HELPER call at 3824e01e (0x3815bc38), whose rv the wrapper finds not in
  {0,3,6} and treats as fatal.  That helper -- a 476-byte-frame libxpc routine --
  is the true frontier, and it is reached BEFORE the loop's trap ever fires in
  the observed run (or the loop's success/failure rv is the helper's).
