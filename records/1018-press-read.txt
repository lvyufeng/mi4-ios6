## 4. WHAT THE SAMPLER SAW — and why it is NOT the decider

    xnu_live_stall_pc   10 samples, last at capture :32958 (= BEFORE the loop begins at :33993)
    distinct pcs        0x2be88420, 0x80010219, 0x8001024d, 0x8001cd5d, 0x8001ce41 (all KERNEL mode)
    last tick            xnu_live_tick_irq=0x00000080 at :28759 — the tick ladder stops BEFORE the loop

The sampler fires on the timer tick's powers-of-two ladder and reads the INTERRUPTED context
(src/platform/darwin13/pe_msm8974.c:529). Once the CPU is wedged in the USER-mode trap loop the tick
no longer runs, so the sampler's last sample PREDATES the loop and reads a kernel pc. **The sampler
cannot witness this trap** — which is why the reader must decide the loop from `_seq`/`_pc`, exactly
as design §1.1 says, and not from a sampler intersection.

## 5. THE READER DEFECT THIS RUN EXPOSED (fixed this step)

tools/check_1018_capture.py first returned **U-SILENT** on this capture — a falsification of 1017
produced by the READER, not the device. Its loop test required the witness pc to intersect the STALL
SAMPLER's pcs; when it did not, it fell to U-SILENT. That is [[mi4-a-claim-in-a-comment-is-not-a-check]]
in the reader: the design's §1.1 criterion is `_seq` climbing over an invariant `_pc`, and the sampler
was only ever corroboration. The checker now:
  * decides U-FIRES-LOOPS from `_seq` climbing (>=2) over a SINGLE invariant detail `_pc`;
  * reports the sampler intersection as COUNTS in the cell, not as the decider;
  * keeps U-SILENT reachable only when the witness did NOT loop AND never read a sampled pc.
Two self-test cases lock it: `U-FIRES-LOOPS-nosampler` (a climbing invariant pc the sampler never
reads -> LOOPS) and `U-SILENT-single` (ONE such pc, no climb -> SILENT). Selftest PASS; make check 0.

## 6. THE FRONTIER, NAMED

**pid 1 (`launchd`) is re-executing the Thumb `udf #254` at `__os_alloc_slow`+0x108, in USER mode,
8183 published times** — `sleh_undef`'s user arm is NOT terminating the process. The `udf` is reached
only when `__os_alloc_slow`'s `_mach_vm_map` returns non-zero (1017 §3.2), so the allocator's ring
mapping is being REFUSED and then the trap path loops instead of delivering `EXC_BAD_INSTRUCTION`.

**Next rung (1019):** witness `_mach_vm_map`'s RETURN VALUE and its map ARGUMENTS as pid 1's
allocator makes them — the SHREG/`map_file` territory of 998. Two sub-questions: (a) WHY does the map
fail; (b) WHY does `sleh_undef`'s user arm return-and-re-trap instead of `exception_triage`.
Design: docs/1019-design.md (in progress).

## 7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CLAIM

- **CONFIRMS:** 1017's `udf #254` fires dynamically; the arm's direct channel works; the mech is a
  return-and-re-trap LOOP (1017 §3.3 mechanism (i)), not a one-shot transition and not a crash.
- **does NOT claim:** iOS 7 userspace launched. The boot reached pid 1's allocator and wedged there.
- **does NOT claim** the loop is the run's ONLY frontier — it is where the console FILLED (2 MB),

  the process the design was built to name.
